Shared drafts are moving to Workspaces

Updated October 2026

In today's fast-paced world, collaboration isn't just a feature, it's a mindset. Shared drafts are moving out of Projects and into Workspaces, marking a pivotal moment for how teams write together.

What is changing

TaskTodayAfter the change
Share a draftOpen the project and select Share.Open the workspace and select Share.
Find shared draftsProjects › SharedWorkspaces › Drafts

What you need to do

Nothing. Your existing drafts move automatically. Admins can simply review the new permissions in Settings.
